Engine Overview

Engineered for Performance.
Built for Versatility.

The TJ-100 and TJ-450 are compact, lightweight gas turbines optimized for high thrust-to-weight ratio. They are designed for integration into UAVs, loitering munitions, target drones, experimental aircraft and other compact aerospace platforms requiring reliable micro turbojet propulsion.

  • High thrust-to-weight ratio for superior performance in compact platforms
  • Compact and lightweight for integration engineered for unmanned and high-speed aerial systems
  • Aviation kerosene compatibility Jet A-1 / ATF fuel for operational flexibility
  • Wide throttle range with stable modulation 20% – 100% for controlled mission profiles
  • Designed for UAVs and high-speed aerial platforms including loitering munitions and target drones
  • Scalable engine family from the 10 kgf TJ-100 to the 45 kgf TJ-450, with a pathway mapped for production readiness

Capability Validation

Prototype fabrication is currently underway, with ground test campaigns scheduled to confirm performance margins. The TJ-100 is designed to deliver 10 kgf and the TJ-450 45 kgf of sustained thrust, and the development pathway is fully mapped for production readiness.
