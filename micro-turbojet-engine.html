TJ-100 Micro Turbojet Engine

Compact. Powerful. Mission Ready.

The TJ-100 is a compact Micro Turbojet Engine designed for unmanned aerial platforms and other high-speed aerospace applications. It delivers 10 kgf of reliable thrust on aviation kerosene with a high thrust-to-weight ratio.

  • 10 kgf Static Thrust (98 N)
  • Aviation Kerosene Fuel Type (Jet A-1 / ATF)
  • 20% – 100% Stable Modulation Throttle Range
  • Built for Missions UAVs, Loitering Munitions, Target Drones & Testbeds

Engine Overview

Engineered for Performance.
Built for Versatility.

The TJ-100 is a compact, lightweight gas turbine optimized for high thrust-to-weight ratio. It is designed for integration into UAVs, loitering munitions, target drones, experimental aircraft and other compact aerospace platforms requiring reliable micro turbojet propulsion.

  • High thrust-to-weight ratio for superior performance in compact platforms
  • Compact and lightweight for integration engineered for unmanned and high-speed aerial systems
  • Aviation kerosene compatibility Jet A-1 / ATF fuel for operational flexibility
  • Wide throttle range with stable modulation 20% – 100% for controlled mission profiles
  • Designed for UAVs and high-speed aerial platforms including loitering munitions and target drones
  • Scalable platform for future higher-thrust variants development pathway mapped for production readiness

Capability Validation

Prototype fabrication is currently underway, with ground test campaigns scheduled to confirm performance margins. The TJ-100 is designed to deliver 10 kgf of sustained thrust on aviation kerosene, and the development pathway is fully mapped for production readiness.
