Our Propulsion Systems

RJT-02 Ramjet Engine

When Subsonic is No Longer Enough.

When subsonic is no longer enough, the RamJet Engine turns your platform into a supersonic predator. Engineered with zero moving parts for battlefield reliability, this annular module ignites at Mach 0.9 and propels you past Mach 1.5 with over 2 kN of sustained thrust.

Ramjet engines are widely used in aerospace applications including supersonic missiles, high-speed target drones, and hypersonic test vehicles. The current design serves as a scalable baseline; with further development, higher thrust variants can be realized for larger platforms and extended supersonic missions.

Engine Overview

Supersonic Performance.
Simple. Scalable. Reliable.

The RJT-02 Ramjet Engine is engineered for high-speed propulsion beyond the subsonic regime. Its annular architecture uses zero moving parts and is designed for reliable supersonic operation.

The current design serves as a scalable baseline, with further development enabling higher-thrust variants for larger platforms and extended supersonic missions.

Capability Validation

Simulation Validated. Ready for the Next Phase.

The ramjet combustion cycle and supersonic flow dynamics have been simulation-validated, confirming the design's ability to deliver ~2 kN thrust at Mach 1.5.

The development pathway is established, and the system is now ready to progress to prototyping in the next phase of our internal development schedule.
