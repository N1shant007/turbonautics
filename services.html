Our Services

Engineering Services for High-Performance Aerospace Systems.

01

Thermal Analysis & Heat Transfer

Analysis covering CHT, thermal stress and phase-change behavior across cryogenic to high-temperature conditions.

02

Flow Analysis & CFD

Analysis of rotating machinery using MRF/sliding mesh, multiphase flow, cavitation and compressible/supersonic flow, with OpenFOAM as the primary tool.

03

Combustion & Reactive Flow

Analysis of premixed and non-premixed combustion, emissions, scramjet/supersonic mixing and micro-turbine combustors.

04

Structural & Stress Analysis

Analysis covering static/nonlinear FEA, burst pressure, thermal-structural coupling and custom problems using deal.II.

05

Vibration & Rotor-Dynamic Analysis

Analysis covering modal response, harmonic response, Campbell diagrams and HCF/LCF fatigue.

06

CAD & 3D Surface Modeling

Conceptual design, parametric 3D CAD modeling, and high-fidelity surface development for complex aerospace airframes, turbomachinery components, and structural assemblies.

Turbonautics brings together aerospace engineering, advanced propulsion and unmanned systems under one engineering capability. We combine simulation, analysis, design and development to create high-performance technologies for the next generation of aerospace and defence.
