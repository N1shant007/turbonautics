About Turbonautics

Engineering Services for High-Performance Aerospace Systems.

About Us

Advancing the Boundaries of High-Speed Flight.

Turbonautics is an Indian aerospace startup building indigenous high-speed propulsion systems for unmanned aerial platforms. Incorporated on 12 February 2026 in Bangalore, Karnataka, we are a DPIIT-recognised startup driven by a single mission: to eliminate India's dependence on imported engines for UAVs, cruise missiles, and aircraft.
Our focus is on designing and manufacturing micro-turbojet engines and ramjet engines from scratch, entirely in-house and entirely indigenous. We are not assembling imported parts. We are engineering from first principles, building sovereign capability in critical propulsion technology.

  • Advanced Propulsion & Aerospace Engineering
  • Product Design, Engineering & Manufacturing
  • Mission & High-Speed Aerospace Applications

While our roots are in India, our vision is global. The need for indigenous, high-performance propulsion is universal. Whether you are a defence ministry seeking strategic autonomy, an OEM looking for reliable engine partners, or a system integrator building next-generation platforms — Turbonautics is ready to partner with you.
